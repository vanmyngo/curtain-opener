# Introduction
Curtains opener that is inspired by SwitchBot. Customized to work with own home assistant but also with Amazon Echo and other on the market home assistant. Focus on energy efficiency and quietness.
# Requirements
1. Slides smoothly through the rod, including adjustable rods
2. Detach/attach easily onto the rod without modifying the rod
3. Noise < 35dB, whispers
4. Device does not stand out, should blend in with environment
5. Energy efficient, minimal leakage current on sleep or hold mode
6. Batter-powered and chargeable, ideally by solar energy
7. Matter over Thread, smart home standard and future-proofing
8. User should be able to set curtain to open on sunrise and closes on sunset, according to local time
